**Related backend rows:** —

Status: worker done (T-803): pg_cron queues the `expiry_reminders` job daily at 06:23 UTC (Sentry cron `expiry-reminders`); `app.queue_expiry_reminders` queues one `document_expiring` notify job per document and notice (60, 30, 7 days; key `doc:<id>:<days>`, ids only), which T-602's handler sends as Template 04 and an in-app row; a notice that failed is re-queued by the next daily run, so it is sent once the cause is fixed. Open on #70: who the pack owners are (default: the requester and the people who recorded a registration citing the document), reminders for a registration's own expiry (default: none; no template or kind exists) and the document display name, without which the notify job fails visibly (T-602). `H04.main` is tested in worker/tests/test_expiry_reminders_pg.py, worker/tests/test_expiry_reminders.py and supabase/tests/app/99_expiry_reminders.test.sql.
