| Domain | Register status | Scope | Milestone |

**Related backend rows:** —

Status: built in T-505. A revision is uploaded on S-NV2-002 ("Upload a new version": app.register_artwork; only a product's first artwork counts, so a revision is free even with the allowance used) and checked from the screen's closing bar, "Run the check" (apps/web/src/components/checks/RunCheck.tsx), which calls app.request_check and opens Check in progress (S-NV2-024, T-1111), which follows the job in app.jobs until "Open the report". Since #274 the check covers the product's markets in the caller's scope, so a market-scoped editor checks their own markets and follows their own job (tested in supabase/tests/app/99_market_scope_check_envelope.test.sql and the `[#274]` e2e in recheck-coverage.spec.ts). Earlier checks stay as written (insert-only); the report shows each market's latest check job. `D08.main`, `D08.must.1` and `D08.must.2` are tested in apps/web/tests/e2e/recheck-coverage.spec.ts (a real revision extracted and checked by the worker), the bar's logic in apps/web/src/lib/checks/run.test.ts. Open on #55: a list of earlier checks (no design shows one; history is kept and tested in the database). The check-in-progress screen S-NV2-024 is built in T-1111 (D04). From T-1114 the report itself (Fix mode, S-FIX-001) leads to a re-check. For an editor, Upload revision opens Upload & scope. While a check job of the product is in flight, a banner reads "Revision uploaded." and links to Check in progress. A check against an older ruleset offers "Re-check against …" (app.request_check). `?job=<check job id>` opens an earlier result read-only, as "A newer result exists.", so earlier checks stay readable (BE-05). `D08.main`, `D08.must.1` and `D08.must.2` are also tested in apps/web/tests/e2e/report.spec.ts and apps/web/src/lib/report/fix.test.ts.
