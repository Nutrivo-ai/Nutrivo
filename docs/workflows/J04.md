Status: built in T-902. S-MIS-001 and S-MOB-006 (`/w/[workspace]/alerts`) read `app.alerts` under RLS with the person's own `app.alert_reads` and show one card per rule change, marked coming or in force with its date, naming the recipes whose latest check used the rule (contract 5, written by T-901). A card's current phase is per product × market (the sweep's in_force row once written), so it comes back unread when the change comes into force. Filters (All, In force, Coming) live in the URL, and Mark all read calls `app.mark_alerts_read` for the person only (BE-39). A recipe links to its finding only in a market whose region the workspace's plan holds (`entitled_regions`); elsewhere it is named, with the reason, and cannot be opened. S-MIS-002 (`/w/[workspace]/horizon`) is led by the nearest withdrawn-authorisation deadline, then lists the coming changes, sell-through ends and `ref.horizon_items` not yet in force in date order; a draft or proposal carries no countdown (BE-37). `J04.main` and `J04.must.1` are tested in apps/web/tests/e2e/alerts.spec.ts and apps/web/src/lib/alerts/model.test.ts. Open on #73 (`needs-human`): horizon items have no market or link to recipes, so their recipe counts and the inbox's Draft cards are not built (gap 1); the "What we watch ahead of law" panel (gap 2); the Documents tab, which is H04 data (gap 3); and gap 4: J04.must.1 is enforced by the web server (apps/web/src/lib/plan/regions.ts), not by the database. The verdict, the label report, the board's findings view and the artwork page's panel states neither read nor show a finding in a region the plan does not hold; they show the not-in-plan state instead, and a direct URL gets the same. The RLS on checks and findings still keys on membership scope, so the database guard and the worker's report PDFs and exports are proposals on #73.
