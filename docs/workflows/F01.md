Status: built in T-506. S-NV2-006 (`/w/[workspace]/board`) reads under RLS every product that is not discontinued and its markets: a market cell is `app.product_nodes.display_state` with its open actions, and markets are grouped by region, each group led by a region cell that is `app.rollup_state(product, region)` as the database returns it, read for the whole board in one call through `app.board_rollup(workspace)` (incident #315; it answers `rollup_state` for every product × region under the viewer's RLS, and each product's worst state across its regions) (a region not read, or a value outside the four states, is Not assessed; a market the product is not sold in has no state). The row says which ruleset it was judged against (current, stale or never run); filters (Action needed, Not assessed, Stale, species) are links in the URL. The rows are paged, 50 a page in name order, after the filters (`?page=N`, a link like the filters; a filter link starts again at page 1; a page past the end shows the last), with "Rows a–b of n" and Previous/Next under the grid. The summary counts every product. abnutrivo decided this on 25 Nov 2026 (issue 311): at 300 recipes the full grid was 2,400 cells and 4 MB, against the 1,500 ms budget. It answers, for the MVP, the S-MIS-003 question of how a 300-recipe board ships; search and saved views stay F02, LATER.
