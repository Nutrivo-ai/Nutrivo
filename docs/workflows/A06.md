# A06 · Remove a member

| Domain | Register status | Scope | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Access | DRAWN | BUILD | M1 |

**Actor · trigger:** Admin  
**Design:** Workspace Settings (People, Your account); Onboarding Flow; Account Recovery

## Steps
1. app.remove_member (to build) sets ended_at and the reason.
2. Signing grants are revoked by trigger.
3. A 'session_revoke' job signs the person out.
4. Audit.

## Database (Workflow Register)
memberships update ended_at; signing_grants update revoked_at; auth.sessions revoke; audit_events insert

**Depends on:** A05, A08, O01

## Must hold
- Past events read exactly as before (BE-06).
- Access ends on the next request.

## Failure and edge cases
- Removing the last admin is refused.
