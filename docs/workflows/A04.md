# A04 · Accept an invitation

| Domain | Register status | Scope | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Access | DRAWN | BUILD | M1 |

**Actor · trigger:** Invitee · opens the link  
**Design:** Workspace Settings (People, Your account); Onboarding Flow; Account Recovery

## Steps
1. Sign in or sign up.
2. app.accept_invitation(token): hash match, not expired, revoked or used; the email must match.
3. The membership copies role, scope, external flag and end date.
4. Audit membership.created with the external flag stamped.

## Database (Workflow Register)
invitations read, mark accepted; auth.users insert or link; memberships insert; audit_events insert (membership.created, external flag stamped)

**Depends on:** A03

## Must hold
- One-time and email-bound.
- The external flag is stamped at write time (BE-06).

## Failure and edge cases
- Expired: P0040 and the expired-invite page.
- Different email: P0041.
- Already a member: P0042.
