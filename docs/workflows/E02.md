# E02 · Compute the compliance envelope

| Domain | Register status | Scope | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Briefs | DRAWN | BUILD | M10 |

**Actor · trigger:** System  
**Design:** Nutrivo 2.0 (new product brief)

## Steps
1. Evaluate the formula and claims against accepted rules (additive_condition, threshold, prescribed_wording).
2. Required particulars, permitted and prohibited wording per language, limits, obligations, withheld items with gaps.
3. Cached by inputs_hash.

## Database (Workflow Register)
envelopes insert (ruleset_version, inputs_hash); envelope_items insert (kind, rule_id, provenance)

**Depends on:** K05, K03 term register, G01

## Must hold
- The same honesty as D04: anything not encoded is withheld with its gap.
- Nutrient adequacy stays out of scope until FEDIAF is licensed.
