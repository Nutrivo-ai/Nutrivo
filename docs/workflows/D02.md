# D02 · Triage panels

| Domain | Register status | Scope | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Label checks | DRAWN | BUILD | M3 |

**Actor · trigger:** Editor  
**Design:** Nutrivo 2.0 (label report, artwork pins, coverage node); Mobile; Empty and Error States

## Steps
1. Detected panels and languages shown over the artwork.
2. A person corrects a language or marks a panel not assessable with a reason.

## Database (Workflow Register)
artwork_panels update status; check_findings insert (not_assessed, reason_code)

**Depends on:** D01

## Must hold
- A panel that cannot be assessed produces Not assessed findings with a reason code, never silence.

**Related backend rows:** —

Status: step 1 and the must-hold built in T-304; step 2 open on #42. S-NV2-025 (`/w/[workspace]/products/[product]/artwork/[artwork]`) lists every detected panel with its language and, in the design's fifth column, its state over each market's latest check (`app.checks` by `panel_id`, worst first: action, then not assessed, then clear; "Not checked" in grey before any check). Once checked, the state links to the label report. A not_assessable panel always reads Not assessed with its reason code (its `failure_code` first, then the reason codes of its checks' Not assessed findings), before and after a check, and the engine (T-403) withholds its findings with that code. `D02.main` and `D02.must.1` are tested in apps/web/src/lib/artwork/describe.test.ts and apps/web/tests/e2e/triage.spec.ts (a real check through `app.request_check` and the worker). Open on #42: step 2 (correct a language, mark a panel not assessable) needs a db RPC, a reason code for a person's choice and a design control, so the screen draws no correction control yet.
