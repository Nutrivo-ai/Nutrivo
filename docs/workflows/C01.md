# C01 · Create a product and family

| Domain | Register status | Scope | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Products | DRAWN | BUILD | M4 |

**Actor · trigger:** Editor  
**Design:** Nutrivo 2.0 (board, product)

## Steps
1. Create or pick a family (the billing unit).
2. Create the product: stage, species, life stage, format, operator.
3. Choose markets: product_nodes rows start not_assessed / unknown.
4. Optional formula lines.

## Database (Workflow Register)
families insert (billing unit); products insert (stage, family_id, operator); product_nodes insert per market; formula_lines insert

**Depends on:** B01, M02 (family count)

## Must hold
- Products are never deleted (BE-13).
- Paid plans stop at the overage cap and offer the next plan (BE-34).

## Failure and edge cases
- Over the cap: next-plan offer, never a larger invoice.

## Acceptance IDs (each needs a test carrying the ID in its title; docs/19)
- `C01.main` Create a product and family: the main path works end to end
- `C01.must.1` Products are never deleted (BE-13).
- `C01.must.2` Paid plans stop at the overage cap and offer the next plan (BE-34).
- `C01.fail.1` Over the cap: next-plan offer, never a larger invoice.

**Related backend rows:** BE-13, BE-34

Status: built in T-401, overage cap in T-704. `app.create_product` creates the family (unless one is picked), the product and its markets at not_assessed / unknown, audited; S-NV2-022 Add a product (`/w/<ws>/products/new`). Products have no delete. On a monthly Basic or Standard plan a recipe past the paid ones is overage while the recurring net amount stays within 1.5x list (€374 / €749, D-03, BE-34): the `recipe_overage` job raises the recipe add-on in Stripe and re-mirrors it. Past the cap, on an annual term, or before the mirror carries the amounts, a new family is refused with P0034 and the screen offers the next plan. Tests: supabase/tests/app/70_products_families.test.sql, 61_overage_and_dunning.test.sql, worker/tests/test_overage_dunning_pg.py, apps/web/tests/e2e/products.spec.ts, src/lib/products/rules.test.ts. Open on #65: what counts toward the cap, annual terms, proration, lowering the add-on.
