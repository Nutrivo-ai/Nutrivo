**Related backend rows:** BE-36

Status: built in T-702 and T-704. Step 1 (T-702): the stripe-webhook Edge Function verifies Stripe's signature and app.record_stripe_event stores each event once by id (app.payment_events, insert-only) and queues an ids-only billing_sync job; the worker re-reads the subscription from Stripe and applies it through app.apply_subscription; a daily billing_reconcile job (pg_cron 03:17 UTC, Sentry cron `stripe-reconcile`) repairs missed events. A failed payment moves the workspace to `dunning` (nothing restricted) and a paid one back to `active`. Steps 2 and 3 (T-704): pg_cron queues one `dunning` job a day (07:41 UTC, Sentry cron `dunning`); day 0 is the invoice's first invoice.payment_failed event; the worker re-reads each due invoice from Stripe, retries the payment on days 3 and 7, and app.record_dunning_step records the step once per invoice and day in app.dunning_steps (insert-only, admins read), queues the payment_failed email for days 0, 3, 7 and 14, and on day 14 moves `dunning` to `new_artwork_stopped`, which stops new artworks only. Nothing is deleted and public.verify_reference is untouched. `M04.main` and `M04.must.1` are tested in worker/tests/test_overage_dunning_pg.py, test_overage_dunning.py, test_billing_pg.py, test_billing.py, test_stripe_webhook_function.py and supabase/tests/app/60_billing_webhook.test.sql, 61_overage_and_dunning.test.sql. Decided on #65 on 7 Nov 2026: Nutrivo retries (Smart Retries off, a founder's Stripe dashboard step); emails go to the Stripe customer email, and a founder reads the copy before G8; the day-7 admin banner is a follow-up web task; no collection after a cancellation (the default in force; abnutrivo keeps it open as a commercial question).
