**Related backend rows:** BE-36

Status: partly built in T-702. Step 1 is done: the stripe-webhook Edge Function verifies Stripe's signature and app.record_stripe_event stores each event once by id (app.payment_events, insert-only) and queues an ids-only billing_sync job; the worker re-reads the subscription from Stripe and applies it to app.subscriptions and app.workspaces.plan / status through app.apply_subscription; a daily billing_reconcile job (pg_cron 03:17 UTC, Sentry cron `stripe-reconcile`) repairs missed events. A failed payment moves the workspace to `dunning` (nothing restricted) and a paid one back to `active`; cancellation returns it to the free plan and deletes nothing. Steps 2 and 3, the notices and retries on days 3 and 7 and the day-14 stop, are the dunning schedule's (T-704). `M04.main` and `M04.must.1` are tested in worker/tests/test_billing_pg.py, test_billing.py, test_stripe_webhook_function.py (supabase/functions/stripe-webhook/webhook.test.mjs) and supabase/tests/app/60_billing_webhook.test.sql; open choices on #62.
