About

We check pet food labels against the law of every market you sell in

The European Union, the United Kingdom and the United States including every state, in the language each panel is printed in. Composition, additives, mandatory particulars, claims, character heights measured against the legal minimum, and every registration you owe with the authority and the deadline.

What makes it worth trusting is that every answer is auditable. Each finding cites the article it came from and says whether a rule decided it or a judgement did, and we publish how much of each market we hold. A compliance answer you cannot check is not an answer.

Where we stop, deliberately

The line we will not sell past

We name the obligation, the authority, the route, the evidence, the fee and the deadline, and we fill in everything your product record already answers. Then you file it. Not because we are being careful with our liability, but because only the operator named on the label is entitled to.

Every competitor in this category is under pressure to blur that line, because "we handle your registrations" sells better than "we prepare them". It is not on our roadmap at any price.
