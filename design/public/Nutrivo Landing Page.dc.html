The roll-up rule

A region never shows as Clear while one of its markets is not. If Texas is blocked, the US does not read clear because the other forty-nine are — you see the failing node, named.

Why a region is sold whole

You cannot buy a single state or a single member state, because a state is an edition of a rulebook rather than a rulebook of its own. Buy the region and you have all of it.
