{{ nv }}

Unlimited re-checking is priced into every plan and is only affordable if a re-check re-runs the rules and language panels that actually changed. Unscoped, Standard's margin falls from 93% to 77%. Scoped re-check must ship with the diff engine, not after it.

The free allowance · three label artworks per workspace

The whole product on each of the three, sending included. No card and no clock. The counter increments on a new artwork only — a re-check, a further market inside the same region, and a revised version of the same artwork are all free. When the three are used the workspace refuses a new artwork and changes nothing else: everything already checked stays readable, re-checks stay free, and every shared document keeps verifying. Enforce it per workspace, since the terms forbid creating workspaces to farm it.
