And one flag: external

A consultant is not a fifth role. They are an editor or a viewer, scoped, marked external. Being outside your company is a fact about the person, not a set of permissions, and it changes four things.

A supplier is not a person here at all

Suppliers and contract manufacturers get a scoped upload link with no account, so they are not on this screen and not marked external. You are never charged for their access, and they never see anything but the request.

Signer is not a role either

Signing authority attaches to a named person for named markets and is granted on its own screen. Changing somebody's role never grants or removes it, and an external person is never offered it.

Removing somebody

Their access ends immediately. Their name stays on every check they ran and every document they signed, because the history is append-only and a record with a hole in it is not a record.

Nobody holds signing authority for US · Texas

A Certificate of Check cannot be issued for a market without a named signatory, so the Texas registration evidence pack cannot be signed. Reports of Check are unaffected — they are attested by nobody by design.

Closing this workspace

It goes dormant rather than being deleted. Nobody can sign in, nothing new can be checked, and every report already sent stays verifiable — because somebody outside your company may still be holding one.

How long we keep things, and why some of it is permanent

A report you sent may be held by a retailer, an authority or an artwork agency who has no account with us. If our record of it could be deleted, their copy would become unverifiable, so the parts a report was pinned to are kept for good.
