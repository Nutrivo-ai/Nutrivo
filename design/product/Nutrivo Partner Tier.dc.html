The one added question

Whose product is this? A consultant's first recipe belongs to a client, so the client workspace has to exist before the recipe does. It is one field with a create-new option, and it is the only structural difference in the whole flow.

And the one moved

The responsible operator is asked per client rather than per recipe, because a client's operator is the same across their whole range. Enter it once when you add the client, and every recipe in that workspace inherits it.
