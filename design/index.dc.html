The identity, in one paragraph

Cobalt

Near-black ink with one bright blue used strictly for interaction, on white. Instrument Sans for headings and body, IBM Plex Mono as the machine layer for references, dates and codes. Square corners, hairline rules, registration marks on framed objects. The full specification is in the brand file.
